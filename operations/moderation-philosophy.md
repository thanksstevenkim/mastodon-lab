# Moderation Philosophy

## Purpose

Moderation exists to protect the community and define the scope of responsibility for this instance.

Its purpose is not to change people's beliefs or personalities.

Users who disagree with the rules are free to participate elsewhere within the Fediverse.

## Principles

- Moderation protects the community.
- Moderation does not attempt to reform users.
- Consistency is more important than severity.
- Every moderation action should be explainable.
