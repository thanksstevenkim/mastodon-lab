# Federation Governance

## Administrator Conflicts

Disagreements between instance administrators cannot always be resolved through moderation tools.

Because both parties possess administrative authority, conflicts may quickly escalate beyond technical moderation.

## Guiding Principles

- Avoid emotional escalation.
- Remove inappropriate statements made by this administrator when necessary.
- Distinguish personal disagreement from instance-level decisions whenever possible.

## Lessons Learned

The public image of an instance is often closely associated with its administrator.

Administrative behavior therefore affects trust in the instance itself.
